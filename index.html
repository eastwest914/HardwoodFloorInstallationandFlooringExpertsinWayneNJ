Flooring Questions We Hear Often

Can you refinish or resurface my existing hardwood?

Often, yes. Wood resurfacing can bring tired floors back to life when the boards have enough wear layer left. We look at the floor and tell you if it is a good candidate.


What is luxury vinyl tile?

Luxury vinyl tile, often called LVT, is a durable flooring that imitates wood or stone. It is a popular choice for kitchens, basements and busy family spaces.


Do you install laminate floors?

Yes. Laminate is a budget-friendly way to get a wood look, and we handle the installation from preparation to trim.


Can I buy carpet and rugs from you?

Yes. We offer carpet for sale along with installation, and area rugs to finish a room.
